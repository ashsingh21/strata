Shor Sign up to try it

A music app that teaches you while you use it

I'm making Shor for people who want to make music and don't know where to start. You open a lesson, and it walks you through a real song one step at a time. Each step checks itself, so you know when you've got it right before moving on.

What's in it

46 lessons so far, all running inside the app:

Basics3 lessons
A first beat, a bassline and chords.
Theory10 lessons
Octaves, scales, keys, major and minor, intervals by ear, triads, progressions, writing a melody over chords, 7th chords and raag basics.
Sound design13 lessons
How the synth works, then recipes for a deep bass, pad, flute, Indian harp, tanpura, shehnai, lead and lo-fi keys.
Songs13 lessons
A house track and a lo-fi beat built from scratch, a Bollywood lo-fi melody, and walkthroughs of two finished songs.
Sound match7 lessons
Hear a sound, look at its spectrum and rebuild it.

Around the lessons it's a normal music app: tracks and clips, a synth, a drum kit, EQ and compression, recording, and WAV export. What you learn here carries over to other music software.

Indian music

The piano roll can name notes in sargam, and raags like Bhairav, Todi and Malkauns are in the key menu next to major and minor. There are lessons on building a tanpura sound and on raag basics, and a Bollywood lo-fi project.
